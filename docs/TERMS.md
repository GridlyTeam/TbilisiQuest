## 2. Safety — read this part

The app sends you walking to real places in a real city. **Your safety is your
responsibility, and no offer in this app is worth risking it for.**

- **Never look at your phone while crossing a road.** Stop walking before you
  read the screen.
- The app pauses above walking speed. **Do not use it while driving, cycling, or
  riding in any vehicle**, whether you are the driver or not.
- Play runs in daylight hours only.
- Do not enter private property, building sites, roads, tunnels, rail land,
  riverbanks or any closed area to reach a venue. Every drop is at a shop with a
  public entrance; if reaching one seems to require anything else, you are in
  the wrong place — report it.
- Obey traffic law and the instructions of police and emergency services.
- In an emergency call **112**.

We may suspend accounts that show signs of unsafe play.

## 3. Vouchers

- A voucher is a **discount offered by the venue**, not by us. The venue decides
  what it covers and serves you. We are not the seller of anything you buy.
- Claiming a voucher **holds it for 30 minutes**. If you do not redeem it in the
  shop within that time, it returns to the pool for other players.
- Redeeming requires you to be **physically inside the venue's radius** and to
  scan the code shown at the counter. There is no remote redemption.
- One voucher per person per drop.
- Vouchers have no cash value, cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for
  money, and expire at the time shown.
- If a venue refuses to honour a valid voucher, report it in the app. We will
  take it up with them; we cannot force them to serve you.
